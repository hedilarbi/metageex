Monitoring

Strategy & Segment Control

The backoffice handles broad segments (Habits, Fidelity, Reactivation). Each segment contains specific, nuanced strategies.

S08: Prevent Churn

Based on personal cadence (e.g. 80% of cadence, between 10-17 days).

S09: Recover Abandoned

For new clients (1-2 orders), recency of 18 to 29 days.

S10: Prolonged Abandonment

Recency of 30 to 59 days. Respects the S09 cooldown.
